# Global Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing global sales, profitability,
product performance, customer segments, and discount and pricing trends.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to explore financial and sales data across
different countries, products, and customer segments.

The dashboard provides interactive analysis of:

- Sales and profit performance
- Profit margins
- Product performance
- Customer segments
- Country and market performance
- Discount and pricing patterns
- Sales volume

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Global Sales & Profit Analysis

Provides an overview of overall business performance, including:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Sales trends over time
- Sales and profit by country
- Interactive country filtering

### 2. Product & Market Analysis

Explores product and market performance through:

- Top 5 products by sales
- Top 5 products by profit
- Product profitability
- Sales vs profit analysis
- Country-level comparisons

### 3. Customer Segment Analysis

Examines performance across customer segments using:

- Sales by customer segment
- Profit by customer segment
- Profit margin by segment
- Sales vs profit by segment
- Segment performance across countries

### 4. Discount & Pricing Analysis

Investigates the relationship between:

- Discount bands
- Sales
- Profit
- Profit margins
- Pricing
- Sales volume

### 5. Product Details

Provides detailed product-level analysis using:

- Sales
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Country
- Customer Segment

The page also uses drill-through functionality for detailed analysis.
